Common questions

The drive stopped recording when my phone locked. In the iPhone app, allow location access "While Using the App" or "Always" (Settings, Dragon Drive, Location) and keep Precise Location on. In a web browser, keep Dragon Drive open with the screen on, because browsers pause GPS when the phone locks.

How accurate are my times? A phone's GPS updates about once a second, so 0 to 60 and quarter-mile times from a phone are estimates. A 10 to 25 Hz device like a RaceBox gives accurate times.

Who can see my drives? Each drive is Followers, Everyone or Only me. Change it on the drive's page, or set the default on your profile page. See the Privacy Policy.

How do I report or block someone? Tap Report on any drive, comment, post or route, or Report or Block on their profile. We review every report within 24 hours.

How do I delete my account? Profile page (You), Account, Delete account. It removes everything permanently. If you can't sign in, email us from the address you used.

I didn't get the sign-in email. Check spam, wait a minute and try again. The email has a code you can type into the app instead of tapping the link.
