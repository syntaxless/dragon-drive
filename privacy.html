Dragon Drive

Privacy Policy

Last updated November 17, 2026

Dragon Drive logs your drives: the route, speed, elevation, g-force and timed runs. This page explains what that means for your data. The short version: your drives stay on your phone unless you create an account, you choose who sees each shared drive, we don't sell anything about you, and there are no ads or trackers in the app.

What we collect

DataWhenWhy
Location (GPS position, speed, altitude)Only while you're recording a drive, or when you open the route plannerTo draw your route and work out your stats. Saved with the drive on your phone, and uploaded only if you're signed in and the drive isn't set to Only me.
Motion sensors (accelerometer)While recordingTo measure g-force. Stored as numbers in the drive, never as raw sensor logs.
Email addressWhen you create an accountTo send your sign-in code and link. We don't email you anything else.
Profile: @handle, name, profile photoWhen you set themSo friends can find and follow you. Visible to other signed-in users.
Photos of drives and vehicles, garage descriptionsWhen you add themShown with your drives and garage. Kept on your phone, and synced to your account if you're signed in.
Kudos, comments, follows, planned routes, reports and blocksWhen you use those featuresTo run the social features and keep the community safe.
Camera and microphoneOnly when you film a driveThe video is saved on your phone and is never uploaded by Dragon Drive. You choose whether to share it.
Bluetooth devices (RaceBox and similar)Only when you connect oneTo read its GPS data during a drive.

Without an account, everything stays on your device and nothing about your drives is sent to us.

Who can see your drives

Each drive has its own setting: Followers (the default), Everyone (anyone signed in to Dragon Drive), or Only me (never uploaded to the social feed). By default the first and last 300 m of a shared route are hidden so it doesn't show where you live; you can change that on your profile page. Private vehicles in your garage are never shown on your profile.

Services we use

  • Supabase stores accounts and synced data (database, sign-in and photo storage).
  • Resend sends the sign-in emails, and lets the Dragon Drive team know when a report is filed.
  • OpenFreeMap serves the map tiles. Like any website, it sees your device's IP address and which map area is loaded.
  • OSRM (project-osrm.org) works out road routes when you plan a route in Roads mode; it receives the stops you tap.
  • Overpass API (OpenStreetMap data) looks up speed limits near you when speed alerts are on; it receives the area around your position.
  • GoatCounter counts visits to the website dragondrive.turboloser.co. It doesn't use cookies or identify you, and it isn't used in the iPhone app.

We don't sell or rent your data, we don't show ads, and we don't use your data to track you across other companies' apps or websites.

How long we keep it

Synced data is kept until you delete it or delete your account. Deleting a drive removes it everywhere it was shared. Copies in our hosting provider's backups are removed as those backups expire.

Deleting your account

Open Dragon Drive, go to your profile page (You), open Account, then Delete account. That permanently deletes your account, profile, drives, photos, garage, routes, kudos, comments and follows from our servers and from that phone. Reports you filed are kept without your name so we can finish handling them. If you can't get into the app, email burnout@turboloser.co from the address you signed up with and we'll delete it for you.

Your rights

You can see and change your data in the app, export any drive as GPX, and delete drives or your whole account at any time. Depending on where you live (for example in the EU, UK or California), you may also have the right to ask for a copy of your data or to object to how it's used. Email us and we'll respond within 30 days.

Children

Dragon Drive isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from them. If you think a child has created an account, email us and we'll delete it.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we'll say so in the app. Questions: burnout@turboloser.co.
